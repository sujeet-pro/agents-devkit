# Anti-patterns: adk-review-pr

- Skipping the confirm-intent gate on a non-trivial task.
- Producing output without validation evidence.
- Letting scope creep replace the user's actual ask.
- Inventing facts, file paths, or commands.
- Routing to two skills at once instead of chaining.
- Promoting working artifacts out of `.temp/` before they are the deliverable.
