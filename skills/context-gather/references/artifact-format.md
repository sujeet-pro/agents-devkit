## Rules

1. Every source gets a section, even skipped ones (with reason).
2. Summaries are 5-15 lines per source. Never paste full bodies (link out instead).
3. Sources table at top is the index.
4. Cross-references at end help the brainstorm-facilitator wire them into requirements.
